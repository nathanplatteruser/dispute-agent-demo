# Prompt 1: Define the Workflow

Given a batch of collections dispute documents, identify:

1. Which dispute types are good candidates for first-pass automated screening
   (e.g. straightforward debt-verification or balance disputes with a clear
   factual reference point)
2. Which dispute types must be routed directly to a human reviewer regardless
   of what the automated check finds (e.g. anything alleging identity theft,
   active litigation, bankruptcy, or a regulatory complaint)

Output: a simple two-column list, "screen first" vs "escalate immediately" ,
with one sentence of reasoning per category.
